MEMBERSHIP APPLICATIONS

Privacy notice

Administrator action required before launch: This is a starter notice, not legal advice. The Forum must confirm the data controller/contact, retention period, and complaint/request process before collecting real applications.

What information is collected?

The membership form may collect your name, email address, telephone number, membership category, location, institution or employer, profession or course, study level, scientific interests, and your reason for applying.

Why is it collected?

The information is intended to help the Forum receive, assess, and respond to membership applications, and contact applicants about the application process. Submission does not itself approve membership; applications are considered under the Forum Constitution.

Who can access it?

Membership application records should be accessible only to authorized Forum administrators who need them to review applications. The public website must not provide public browsing, editing, or deletion access to the application register.

Where is it stored?

When configured, the online form sends application data to the Forum’s Supabase-hosted database. The website must not be advertised as accepting online applications until the database is configured and a test submission has been verified. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through this form.

How long is information kept?

The Forum should define and publish a retention period based on its membership administration needs and applicable law. Records should not be kept indefinitely without a documented reason. The Forum should also establish a process for correcting or deleting data when appropriate.

Your choices and requests

You should be able to contact the Forum to ask about the personal information submitted, request correction, or raise a privacy concern, subject to applicable law. Before launch, the Forum should publish a monitored contact address for privacy requests and explain how such requests are handled.

Updates

This notice should be reviewed when the form, database, purpose of collection, or applicable requirements change. The Forum should date each approved revision.
