Who we are

A shared forum for the scientific community of Sebei.

Sebei Scientists Forum (S.S.F.) is an association bringing together scientists and mathematicians in the Sebei region, including people working and learning across agriculture, biology, chemistry, economics, engineering, food technology, mathematics, medicine, physics and related fields.

Founded on 5 May 2020 by Ben Sorowen as the Sebei Association of Mathematical Scientists (S.A.M.S.), the association adopted the name Sebei Scientists Forum to promote inclusivity.

  • Connect science and mathematics teachers across the region
  • Nurture talented students and emerging STEM professionals
  • Share current research and effective teaching practices
  • Contribute evidence-based ideas to local and regional challenges

Constitutional objectives

What we work toward

01
Discuss pressing science and mathematics challenges and recommend solutions to relevant authorities.
02
Advance science and mathematics and promote interest and enjoyment in these fields.
03
Exchange research findings and effective teaching methods among educators.
04
Organize school outreaches, public lectures and knowledge-sharing activities.
05
Connect teachers and support common examinations for O-Level and A-Level where organized.
06
Encourage talented students and strengthen evidence-based teaching and learning.

Membership

Bring your knowledge. Help shape what comes next.

Apply to join the Sebei Scientists Forum. Applications are submitted to the Forum’s online application database for review by the appropriate Forum leadership in line with the Constitution. Submitting this form is an application, not automatic admission.

Ordinary memberOpen to people interested in furthering the aims of the Association. Annual subscription is determined by the Council.
Student memberFor students attending an institution of learning. The Constitution states that student members are not required to pay a subscription.
Honorary memberFor individuals recognized for significant contributions, following the nomination and approval process in the Constitution. Do not apply through the ordinary form; contact the Forum.

Ordinary and student applications are considered by the Council. Membership privileges and office eligibility follow the Constitution.
