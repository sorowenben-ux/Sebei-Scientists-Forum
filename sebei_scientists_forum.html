Who we are

Science with purpose. Progress through partnership.

The Sebei Scientist Forum is envisioned as a collaborative community where people with scientific knowledge and curiosity can meet, exchange ideas and contribute to sustainable development.

We believe meaningful research starts by listening to communities and grows through collaboration, mentorship and sharing knowledge.

  • Build networks among scientists and emerging researchers
  • Encourage science education and mentorship
  • Support research addressing local challenges
  • Promote innovation and evidence-based decision-making
